Criminal Defense & Litigation

Federal Criminal · White Collar

Federal Wire Fraud & Money Laundering: Financial Sector Executive

Defended a senior investment banking executive facing 14 federal counts of wire fraud, money laundering, and conspiracy in connection with an alleged $90M Ponzi scheme. Challenged the government's forensic accounting analysis and the admissibility of key electronic evidence obtained through a contested warrant.

OutcomeAll 14 counts dismissed at pre-trial. Court ruled government's primary evidence inadmissible. Client fully exonerated.
State Criminal · Violent Crime

First-Degree Murder Charge: Private Client

Represented an individual charged with first-degree murder following a disputed altercation in New York. Conducted a comprehensive forensic defense, retained independent pathology and ballistics experts, and exposed material inconsistencies in key witness testimony during cross-examination.

OutcomeNot guilty verdict returned by jury after 22-day trial. Client acquitted on all counts. Full acquittal.
Corporate Criminal · RICO

RICO Conspiracy: Multi-Defendant Corporate Matter

Lead defense counsel for a corporate client and three individual executives charged under RICO statutes in connection with an alleged bid-rigging conspiracy affecting government procurement contracts valued at over $200M over six years.

OutcomeCorporate client acquitted. Two of three individual clients acquitted outright. Third client received a substantially reduced sentence following successful mitigation advocacy.

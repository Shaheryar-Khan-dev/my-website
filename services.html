Corporate Law

Our corporate law practice represents multinational corporations, private equity firms, emerging-growth companies, and governmental entities in transactions of every scale and complexity. We provide strategic counsel on mergers and acquisitions, corporate governance, securities compliance, joint ventures, restructuring, and commercial contracts. Our attorneys have closed deals in excess of $50 billion across six continents, navigating complex cross-border regulatory frameworks with precision and efficiency.

Key areas include: M&A advisory, due diligence, shareholder agreements, board governance, SEC compliance, and IPO counsel.

Criminal Defense

When freedom is at stake, experience is everything. Our criminal defense team has defended clients in federal and state courts against the full spectrum of charges — from white-collar fraud and RICO offences to violent crimes and drug trafficking. We conduct exhaustive case investigations, challenge unlawful evidence, and build defenses that put the prosecution to its full proof. Our approach is aggressive, methodical, and always focused on the best achievable outcome for our client.

Key areas include: Federal criminal defense, white-collar crime, fraud, DUI/DWI defense, appeals, and pre-charge investigations.

Family Law

Family legal matters demand both legal precision and profound human sensitivity. Our family law team provides compassionate, highly effective representation in divorce proceedings, child custody and support disputes, alimony negotiations, adoption, prenuptial agreements, and domestic relations matters. We approach every case with discretion and with a deep understanding of the emotional complexity our clients are navigating — always striving for resolutions that protect your family's future.

Key areas include: Divorce, child custody, spousal support, property division, adoption, and guardianship.

Immigration Law

In an increasingly complex global regulatory environment, immigration matters require both technical mastery and a comprehensive understanding of international law. Our immigration practice assists corporations, executives, skilled workers, and families with visa applications, work permits, permanent residency, naturalization, deportation defense, and asylum proceedings. We operate across the US, UK, EU, Singapore, and UAE immigration systems with deep expertise and a strong track record of success.

Key areas include: Corporate immigration, EB-1/EB-2 visas, O-1 visas, asylum, deportation defense, and citizenship applications.

Real Estate Law

Whether you are acquiring a landmark commercial property, structuring a real estate investment trust, or resolving a complex title dispute, Summit & Clarke's real estate team provides comprehensive transactional and litigation support across all property classes. Our attorneys advise on residential and commercial acquisitions, construction contracts, leasing, zoning and land-use approvals, foreclosure proceedings, and real estate finance across domestic and international markets.

Key areas include: Commercial acquisitions, lease negotiation, title disputes, REITs, construction law, and zoning matters.

Intellectual Property Law

In today's knowledge economy, your intellectual assets are among your most valuable. Our IP practice provides comprehensive protection and enforcement of patents, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets, and licensing arrangements. We represent inventors, tech companies, media conglomerates, and global brands — from initial registration and prosecution through complex infringement litigation and inter partes review proceedings before the USPTO and international tribunals.

Key areas include: Patent prosecution, trademark registration, copyright enforcement, trade secret protection, and IP licensing.

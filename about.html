Est. 1998

A Legacy Built on Principle

Summit & Clarke Legal Associates was founded in New York City in 1998 by partners Eleanor Summit and James Clarke, two former federal prosecutors who shared a conviction that clients deserved more than competent legal representation — they deserved a genuine advocate.

From a modest two-room office on Lexington Avenue, the firm earned a rapid reputation for its meticulous preparation, fearless courtroom presence, and exceptional results in complex litigation. Within five years, Summit & Clarke had grown to a team of 18 attorneys and expanded into corporate transactional work.

Today, the firm maintains flagship offices in New York, London, Singapore, and Dubai, with over 42 attorneys serving clients across six continents. Despite our global scale, we maintain the personal attention and bespoke service that has always defined our practice.

1998

The Founding

Corporate Practice Launch

The firm expands to corporate law, completing its first major cross-border merger valued at over $2.4 billion.

International expansion begins with the opening of the London office, establishing a European presence and Chambers UK ranking.

Digital Transformation

Summit & Clarke launches its proprietary case management platform, enhancing transparency and client communication.

2024

25 Years of Excellence

The firm celebrates its 25th anniversary, with 1,200+ cases won, 42 attorneys, and a 98% client satisfaction rate.
